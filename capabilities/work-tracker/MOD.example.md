## Capture preferences

Confirm with a single ✅ and the slot if one was booked. Never echo the commitment back in
full — repeating it back reads as doubt that it was heard.

## Scheduling preferences

Deep work in the morning, so long blocks belong before lunch. Two hours is the most that
should be booked in one sitting; anything longer gets split across days.

Never book over sacred time — surface the conflict and offer the next window instead.

## Commitments

Verb-first and short. Anything with a deadline gets the deadline in the phrasing, because that
is the part worth seeing at a glance.
