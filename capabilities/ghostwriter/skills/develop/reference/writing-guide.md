# Writing guide

- Lead with a real problem, failure, tension, or discovery.
- One idea per sentence. Concrete detail beats abstraction.
- Technical credibility belongs inside the story, not in a detached architecture dump.
- Headings carry an argument; avoid labels like “Introduction” and “Conclusion”.
- Decisions include tradeoffs and what was rejected.
- Never invent statistics, dialogue, scenes, customers, or first-person positions.
- A short piece is allowed. Add no length without substance.
