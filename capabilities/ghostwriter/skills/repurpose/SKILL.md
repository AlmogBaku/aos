# Repurpose Content

1. Run {{skill: ghostwriter}}'s successful backlog preflight before any state change. If unavailable or failing, stop. Audit the anchor: it needs a clear travelable argument, at least one standalone atom, and an audience on a requested channel from `{{mod: content_channels}}`. Stop and surface unsupported channels. If audit fails, say it should remain one-and-done.
2. Require `status: approved-manual-publish`. Explicit approval first follows Critique's atomic approval transition; it never bypasses unresolved sensitivity or grounding blockers.
3. Work from the approved `## Evidence map` and source references, not prose alone.
4. Extract content atoms first: a scene, claim, mechanism, quote, framework, or question that stands alone. Keep only atoms supported by the approved sources.
5. For each requested channel, choose the few atoms that fit its reader and intended effect. Do not create every format by default.
6. Adapt hook, length, structure, depth, and CTA to each requested channel. Keep the POV, distinctive vocabulary, and conviction level recognizable.
7. Apply the standalone test: without the anchor, does this derivative make sense, respect the medium, and earn attention on its own? If not, revise or do not create it.
8. Preserve claim meaning. A new fact, anecdote, or position returns to `needs-confirmation`.
9. Write variants into the piece's `## Derivatives` section, with each variant's source atoms and sensitivity/confirmation state.
10. New derivatives return the bundle to `status: review`, `next_action: critique`; critique and a fresh user manual-approval are required before any derivative is manual-publish-ready. Never post or schedule.

- **Blog:** durable narrative and technical depth.
- **LinkedIn:** one sharp narrative with enough context to stand alone.
- **X:** concise post or thread; each post earns its place.
